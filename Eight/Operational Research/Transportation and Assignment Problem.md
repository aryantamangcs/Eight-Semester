**Transportation Problem mainly deals with logistic**
It helps in distribution and transportation of resources from one place to another.

***Things to remember whenever you hear about the transportation and assignment problem***
- Quantity of Available Supplies
- Quantity Demanded

> The main purpose is to transport goods and services from source/origin to destination in such manner that the cost of transportation is minimum

## General Mathematical Model of transportation problem

**Source** -> From where the goods are being transmitted. It is also known as origin.
**Destination** -> To where the goods are being transmitted.
**Cell Cost** -> The cost of transportation per unit. It is represented by Cij (where i is source and j is destination)
**Occupied Cell** -> The cell where commodities are allocated
**Unoccupied Cell** -> The cell where commodities are not allocated.
**Degenerate problem** -> The solution in which the number of occupied cell is not equal to the (m + n -1) where m is no of rows and n is no of columns
**Non-Degenerate problem** -> The solution in which the number of occupied cell is equal to the (m + n - 1) where m is no of rows and n is no of columns

## Types of Transportation Problem

#### 1.1 Balanced Transportation Problem
It the total supply = total demand then, the transportation problem is balanced.

#### 1.2 Unbalanced Transportation Problem
If the total supply != total demand the, the transportation problem is unbalanced.

***NOTE: The transportation problem can only be solved if it is balanced. If it is unbalanced problem, then we need to first make it balanced***

## Solution to Transportation Problem

A transportation problem can be solved using two most familiar methods:
- Simplex method
- Transportation method (This is the method that we are going to focus more on)

The transportation method of solving transportation problem requires the following steps:-
1. Obtaining the initial feasible solution.
2. Testing the optimality of the inital solution.

## Step one (Obtaining the initial feasible solution)
It can be calculated by North-West Corned method, Least Cost method and Vogel's Approximation Method)
