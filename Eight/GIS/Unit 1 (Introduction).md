#### 1. Chapter 1 (What is GIS?)

GIS is a computer-based system that is used to collect, store, manage, analyze and display the geographic spatial data.

It answers questions like:-
- Where is something located?
- What is located nearby?

So A computer system that is used to collect, store, manage, analyze and display geographical data is called GIS.

**Real Life Example**

***Suppose Kathmandu Metropolitan City wants to install new public toilets***

Now they require following data:
- Tourist areas
- Bus Parks
- Schools
- Hospitals etc

And GIS helps them to identify all these things and make good decisions on the basis of data.

**Why GIS is called 'geographic' ?

It is called Geographic because every information that GIS provides has location.

Location means (Latitude, Longitude, Coordinates,Address etc)

**Why GIS is called Informative System ?**

An informative system means 
Input -> Processing -> Storage -> Output.

and GIS does exactly this. 

You input road map -> Storage -> Analyze -> Show Shortcut route.

#### 2. Characteristics of GIS ?

The characteristics of GIS are:
- It works with maps.
- Store huge amounts of data.
- Performs Analysis.
- Helps in decision making.


#### 3. Main Functions of GIS (Very Important)

##### 3.1 Data Capture
GIS collects spatial information with the help of various sources such as:
- GPS
- Satellite images
- Drone images
- Maps

***Example*** - Surveyors collection location informations.

##### 3.2 Data Storage

Collected data must be stored. GIS stores the collected spatial information into their own databases. GIS stores the :-
- Maps
- Roads
- Rivers etc

***Example*** : Google Maps stores billions of road data.

##### 3.3 Data Management

Instead of keeping all data together in simple place, GIS manages the collected and stored data into layers instead of mixing everything together.

***Example:***
Layer 1 -> Roads
Layer 2 -> Buildings
Layer 3 -> Schools

##### 3.4 Data Analysis

This is the biggest function and core strength of GIS. It looks at the managed data and tries to analyse them to find meaning information from the data. It answers following questions:
- Which restaurant is nearby?
- Which road has maximum traffic?

##### 3.5 Visualization

With the help of GIS, we can visualize data in terms of maps, charts, 3D models etc.
And human understands maps faster than the tables.

##### 3.6 Decision Making

The main function of GIS is to help in decision making, After analyzing the spatial information, governments, companies or individuals can make better decisions.

***Example:***
- We can avoid the high traffic road and go through the shortcut or long route to save our time.
