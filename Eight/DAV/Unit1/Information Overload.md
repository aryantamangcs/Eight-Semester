Information overload is a common problem in data visualization. It occurs when too much information is presented at once, making it difficult for users to understand or make decisions.

Information overload is a condition in which a user is presented with more information than they can effectively process, resulting in confusion, slower decision-making, and difficulty identifying important insights

## Causes of Information Overload
- Too much Data
- Too many charts
- Excessive Colors
- Too many labels
- Poor Chart Selection

## Effects of Information Overload
- Confustion
- Slower Decision making
- Reduced Productivity.
- Poor user experience
- Increase chance of mistakes.

## Ways to solve Information Overload
- Simplify the visualization
- Use Data Abstraction
- Choose the right chart
- Limit Colors
- Remove unnecessary Labels
